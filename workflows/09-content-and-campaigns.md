# Workflow 09 — Content & campaigns

## Objective

Plan content and campaign assets aligned to positioning and ICP. Actionable briefs over fluff.

## Inputs

- Positioning (08), ICP (03), channel list from profile
- Compliance / banned claims

## Process

1. Pick one campaign job (e.g., demand discovery interviews, waitlist, demo requests).
2. Define audience, offer, CTA, success **target** (target ≠ result).
3. Outline channel plan and content calendar skeleton.
4. Write briefs for 3–7 assets max for Community Edition focus.
5. Ensure every asset brief lists required proof and blocked claims.
6. Draft copy only where asked; keep drafts approval-gated if they include outreach CTAs.
7. Schema: `schemas/campaign-plan.schema.json`.

## Output format

1. **Objective**
2. **Verified findings**
3. **Evidence / sources**
4. **Recommendation**
5. **Next actions**
6. **Confidence & missing info**

## Guardrails

- No fake social proof or engagement metrics
- No implying results already achieved
- Language follows `output_language`
- Synthetic samples labeled **SYNTHETIC**

## Done when

- Campaign job, CTA, and asset briefs are clear
- Measurement plan points to workflow 10/11
