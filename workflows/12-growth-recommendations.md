# Workflow 12 — Growth recommendations

## Objective

Produce a prioritized growth backlog from verified research, experiments, and analytics. Actionable, evidence-based, confidence-rated.

## Inputs

- Outputs from workflows 02–11 as available
- Business constraints and goals

## Process

1. Inventory what is known vs unknown across the funnel.
2. Generate recommendations as experiments or operating changes.
3. Prioritize with simple impact / confidence / effort notes (qualitative is fine).
4. Tie each recommendation to evidence. If weak evidence, label `inference`.
5. Exclude anything requiring Pro-only automation claims; stay Community-honest.
6. Propose a 2–4 week focus plan with owners (human roles), not agent auto-execution of outreach.
7. Schema: `schemas/growth-backlog.schema.json`.

## Output format

1. **Objective**
2. **Verified findings**
3. **Evidence / sources**
4. **Recommendation** (ordered backlog)
5. **Next actions**
6. **Confidence & missing info**

## Guardrails

- Do not promise outcomes
- Do not recommend auto-sending or buying unverified lead lists as a default
- Do not invent customer success stories
- Respect opt-outs and compliance constraints in the plan
- Synthetic strategy decks labeled **SYNTHETIC**

## Done when

- Top 3 actions are clear for the owner this week
- Each action has a success signal and a kill criterion
