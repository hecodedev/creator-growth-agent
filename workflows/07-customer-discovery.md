# Workflow 07 — Customer discovery

## Objective

Plan and synthesize customer discovery interviews to validate problems, willingness-to-pay hypotheses, and messaging — without inventing quotes.

## Inputs

- ICP and offer hypotheses
- Owner interview notes or transcripts they provide
- Pricing status from profile

## Process

1. Build an interview guide: jobs, pains, current alternatives, triggers, willingness-to-pay probes.
2. If no interviews exist yet, deliver the guide + recruiting plan only.
3. When notes are provided, extract themes. Quote only from provided notes; never invent interviewees.
4. Label each insight: `observed` vs `inferred`.
5. Keep pricing conclusions as **hypotheses** unless owner marks validated.
6. Update recommended ICP/positioning inputs for workflows 03/08.
7. Use `schemas/discovery-synthesis.schema.json` for structured synthesis.

## Output format

1. **Objective**
2. **Verified findings**
3. **Evidence / sources** (interview ids/dates from owner notes)
4. **Recommendation**
5. **Next actions**
6. **Confidence & missing info**

## Guardrails

- No fabricated customers, quotes, or “85% said…”
- Targets for interview count ≠ completed interview results
- Synthetic discovery fixtures must say **SYNTHETIC**

## Done when

- Guide ready and/or synthesis grounded in real notes
- Hypotheses explicitly listed for experiment workflow 10
